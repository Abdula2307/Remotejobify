FAQS

Questions? We have answers.

Quick answers to the things people ask most about RemotifyJobs.

Still unsure?

Start by browsing the jobs. It is free and takes a few seconds.

Do I need an account to find jobs?

No. Anyone can browse and apply to jobs without signing up. An account is only needed if you want to post a job.

Are all the jobs remote?

Yes. RemotifyJobs only lists remote opportunities, so you can work from wherever you are.

How long does a job stay on the site?

Jobs posted by employers stay for 30 days. Jobs we collect automatically from open remote job boards stay for 3 days after they were first posted, so the list is always fresh.

Where do the jobs come from?

Some are posted directly by employers on RemotifyJobs. Others come from open remote job boards. Those always show their source and link back to the original listing.

How do I post a job?

Choose Hire Talent, create a free account, and fill in the job details. You decide how people apply: a website link, WhatsApp or email.

Does RemotifyJobs guarantee a job?

No. We bring jobs and applicants together, but hiring decisions are made by employers. Always check a job carefully, and never pay money to get a job.
